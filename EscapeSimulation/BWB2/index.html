Mission Briefing

BEFORE WE BLINK

A space-time pocket collapse has stranded your squad in an oceanic abyss surrounded by quantum-locked Weeping Angels. Your sightlines are the only barrier keeping reality tethered.

1
Venture & Retrieve: Venture away from the stepping-stone TARDIS Sanctuary to gather 12 floating Firefly Tokens and deposit them into the central Console.
2
The Gaze Paradox: Unencumbered Time Lords freeze Angels caught in their Field of View (FOV). However, carrying a token blinds your FOV (eyes focused on the glowing payload). Allies must watch your blind spots!
3
Survival: Each Time Lord begins with 3 Time Energy (HP). An unobserved Angel touch steals 1 HP and locks that Angel for 10 seconds.
